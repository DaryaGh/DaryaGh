## 💼 Featured Projects

### 🎫 Ticket Management System

A comprehensive ticket management platform developed with Django to manage support workflows, user responsibilities, organizational communication, and ticket lifecycles.

This project has been continuously developed and improved over an extended period, with a strong focus on security, access control, maintainability, and practical business requirements.

**Core Features**

- **Role-Based Access Control:** Four user roles — Super Admin, Admin, Employee, and User — with different permissions and access levels.
- **Ticket Lifecycle Management:** Create, assign, track, update, merge, lock, and manage tickets.
- **Ticket Tracking:** Unique tracking numbers with a structured identification format.
- **Assignment Management:** Assign tickets to employees and manage responsibilities and related activities.
- **Advanced Dashboard:** Role-based dashboards with ticket statistics, task information, and activity summaries.
- **Notification System:** Email, SMS, and push notification infrastructure with configurable notification preferences.
- **User & Account Management:** User profiles, authentication, account security, and password recovery workflows.
- **Security Features:** Access restrictions, ticket visibility rules, and protection of sensitive operations.
- **Activity Tracking:** Track ticket views, seen status, search activity, and important ticket interactions.
- **Data Export:** Excel exports and PDF document generation.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA):** PWA support for an app-like experience.
- **Internationalization:** Persian and English language support.

**Technologies:** Python, Django, SQLite, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, PWA.
